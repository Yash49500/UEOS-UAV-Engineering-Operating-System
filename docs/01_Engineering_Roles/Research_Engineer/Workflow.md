# 1. Purpose

This document defines the standard workflow followed by the Research Engineer within the UAV Engineering Operating System (UEOS).

The objective is to ensure that engineering decisions are supported by structured research, objective evaluation, reproducible analysis, and credible technical evidence.

# 3. Phase 1 – Receive Research Objective

## Objective

Understand the engineering problem that requires investigation.

## Activities

- Review mission objectives.
- Review engineering questions.
- Review system requirements.
- Review project constraints.
- Clarify expected outcomes.

## Deliverables

- Research Objective Summary

---

# 4. Phase 2 – Define Research Scope

## Objective

Determine the boundaries of the research effort.

## Activities

- Define research questions.
- Identify assumptions.
- Identify exclusions.
- Define evaluation criteria.
- Define success criteria.

## Deliverables

- Research Scope Document

---

# 5. Phase 3 – Develop Research Plan

## Objective

Create a structured plan for conducting the research.

## Activities

- Identify information sources.
- Select evaluation methods.
- Plan benchmarking activities.
- Define comparison metrics.
- Estimate research effort.

## Deliverables

- Research Plan

---

# 6. Phase 4 – Collect Technical Evidence

## Objective

Gather reliable engineering information.

## Sources

- Peer-reviewed journals
- Conference papers
- Technical standards
- Manufacturer documentation
- Government publications
- Open-source documentation
- Industrial white papers

## Deliverables

- Evidence Repository
- Source Catalogue

---

# 7. Phase 5 – Conduct Literature Review

## Objective

Understand existing research and identify relevant findings.

## Activities

- Review scientific publications.
- Summarise methodologies.
- Extract key results.
- Identify limitations.
- Compare conclusions.

## Deliverables

- Literature Review Report
- Annotated Bibliography

---

# 8. Phase 6 – Perform State-of-the-Art Analysis

## Objective

Identify the best currently available engineering solutions.

## Activities

- Identify leading technologies.
- Compare industrial and academic approaches.
- Assess technology maturity.
- Identify emerging trends.

## Deliverables

- State-of-the-Art Analysis

---

# 9. Phase 7 – Benchmark Existing Solutions

## Objective

Compare existing solutions using objective engineering metrics.

## Benchmark Criteria

- Performance
- Accuracy
- Reliability
- Weight
- Power Consumption
- Cost
- Complexity
- Scalability
- Maintainability

## Deliverables

- Benchmark Report

---

# 10. Phase 8 – Evaluate Technologies

## Objective

Assess the suitability of candidate technologies.

## Examples

- Cameras
- Sensors
- Flight Controllers
- AI Frameworks
- Navigation Algorithms
- Communication Systems
- Companion Computers

## Evaluation Criteria

- Technical Capability
- Integration Effort
- Resource Requirements
- Availability
- Long-Term Support
- Ecosystem Maturity

## Deliverables

- Technology Evaluation Report

---

# 11. Phase 9 – Perform Trade Studies

## Objective

Select the most suitable engineering solution using structured comparisons.

Each alternative shall be evaluated using:

- Technical Performance
- Cost
- Size
- Weight
- Power Consumption
- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Scalability
- Risk
- Schedule Impact

## Deliverables

- Trade Study Report
- Decision Matrix

---

# 12. Phase 10 – Assess Technical Risks

## Objective

Identify research-related risks before implementation.

## Activities

- Identify technical uncertainties.
- Assess implementation risk.
- Assess maturity risk.
- Assess integration risk.
- Recommend mitigation strategies.

## Deliverables

- Technical Risk Assessment

---

# 13. Phase 11 – Develop Technical Recommendations

## Objective

Provide evidence-based recommendations to engineering teams.

Each recommendation shall include:

- Recommendation
- Supporting Evidence
- Assumptions
- Trade-offs
- Risks
- Confidence Level

## Deliverables

- Technical Recommendation Report

---

# 14. Phase 12 – Update Knowledge Base

## Objective

Preserve research findings for future projects.

## Activities

- Archive reports.
- Record lessons learned.
- Update technology database.
- Store benchmark results.
- Document references.

## Deliverables

- Knowledge Base Update

# 16. Success Criteria

The workflow is complete when:

- Research objectives have been answered.
- Evidence is credible and traceable.
- Alternative solutions have been evaluated.
- Trade-offs are documented.
- Technical risks are identified.
- Recommendations are supported by evidence.
- Findings are documented and reusable.

---

# 17. Best Practices

- Use primary sources whenever possible.
- Verify information across multiple references.
- Prefer quantitative comparisons over subjective opinions.
- Clearly distinguish facts from assumptions.
- Record limitations of the available evidence.
- Keep research reproducible and well documented.
