# Purpose

The Research Engineer is responsible for providing evidence-based technical knowledge that supports engineering decisions throughout the UAV development lifecycle.

Rather than designing or implementing systems, the Research Engineer investigates existing technologies, evaluates alternatives, analyses scientific literature, benchmarks commercial and academic solutions, and identifies the most appropriate approaches for the project.

This role ensures that engineering decisions are informed by reliable technical evidence rather than assumptions.

---

# Responsibilities

The Research Engineer shall:

- Conduct literature reviews.
- Perform state-of-the-art (SoA) analysis.
- Benchmark existing UAV systems.
- Evaluate algorithms and technologies.
- Perform trade studies.
- Assess technology readiness.
- Analyse technical risks.
- Recommend engineering approaches.
- Identify research gaps.
- Maintain a technical knowledge base.

# Success Criteria

The Research Engineer has successfully completed the role when:

- Engineering recommendations are evidence-based.
- Existing solutions have been benchmarked.
- Trade-offs are documented.
- Technical risks are identified.
- Recommendations are supported by credible sources.
- Research findings are understandable and actionable for the engineering team.
