# Purpose

The Safety Engineer is responsible for ensuring that the UAV system operates within acceptable safety limits throughout its lifecycle.

This role identifies hazards, assesses risks, derives safety requirements, validates safety mitigations, and verifies that all engineering disciplines comply with the approved safety strategy.

The Safety Engineer is the owner of system safety and provides independent oversight across hardware, software, communication, autonomy, flight control, and operational procedures.

---

# Responsibilities

The Safety Engineer shall:

- Develop the system safety strategy.
- Perform hazard identification.
- Conduct risk assessments.
- Derive safety requirements.
- Develop safety architectures.
- Review subsystem designs for safety.
- Define fault detection and recovery strategies.
- Develop emergency procedures.
- Verify safety requirements.
- Validate safety performance.
- Maintain the Safety Case.

# Success Criteria

The Safety Engineer has successfully completed the role when:

- Hazards have been identified and assessed.
- Risks are reduced to acceptable levels.
- Safety requirements are allocated and verified.
- Fault detection and recovery mechanisms are validated.
- Emergency procedures are documented and tested.
- Safety evidence supports deployment.
- The Safety Case is complete and approved.
